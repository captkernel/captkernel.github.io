12:10 pm

Tribute to the founders

Kids Planet was founded in 2010 by Mrs Neeta Parmar, and built alongside her by Mr Ranjeet Parmar. The day begins with them.

  1. Before doors open: framed portraits of both founders stand on two easels at one side of the stage, garlanded, with a small table of flowers in front. The band's kit is on the other side. The portraits stay all day; the children perform beside them.
  2. 12:05: the welcome song, as a welcome.
  3. 12:10: the teacher MC introduces the tribute in a few sentences. The Principal lays flowers before each portrait, then the Vice Principal and staff place one flower each. If the hall has a screen, the two photographs appear side by side, with no caption.
  4. 12:15: the school band plays Imagine (2 minutes) to close the tribute. Then straight on to Nursery.
  5. No speech, no minute's silence: it's a children's day.

Making the timings real

Backstage logistics

  1. Stage manager. One teacher in the wings with this page, calling each class from its holding room by the Wings by time. The MC never introduces a class that isn't already in the wings.
  2. One-minute changeovers. The MC speaks the link while one class walks off and the next walks on. Classes exit on one side and enter from the other, so they never meet on the steps.
  3. Sound. One file per class, named in show order (01-Nursery, 02-LKG …), on one laptop with a pen-drive backup.
  4. The band never moves. Its kit is preset at the side of the stage and sound-checked before doors open, so Imagine starts with no setup gap.
  5. Lectern comes in during the play's curtain call and goes out after the awards.
1:57 pm

Farewell: the handover

Class 1 has nothing after the play, so it leaves with Nursery, LKG and UKG. (to confirm)

  1. While Classes 6 to 8 receive their awards, ushers quietly ask parents of Nursery to Class 1 to move to the stage-side area.
  2. Class teachers bring their classes to the wings and then stand at the foot of the side steps.
  3. The teacher MC calls one class at a time: Nursery, LKG, UKG, then Class 1. The children cross the stage, wave to the hall, and go down the steps.
  4. The class teacher hands each child only to their own parent or the registered adult. One class on the steps at a time.
  5. A child not collected waits with the class teacher at the side of the stage, never in the aisle.
1:45 pm

Award ceremony

Basis

First Terminal 2026-27

Total marks from the First Terminal Examination (5 to 20 July). Top three in every class, Nursery to Class 8.
Ties

Shared ranks

Children with the same total share the rank, and each receives a trophy and a certificate. Expect 33 to about 40 awards.
Flow

~65 seconds a class

The MC names the class, its teacher and all three winners. They come up together; the Principal presents; one photo; next class.

An award steward (one teacher) lines up each class's winners in the wings two classes ahead, and keeps the trophy table in class order. This keeps the ceremony to 12 minutes.

Anchors

MCs

Classes 7 and 8 are on stage for the whole play, so no senior can anchor both halves. A teacher leads all day, with two student co-anchors.

Lead MC · teacher

The whole day

The welcome, the tribute, the play, the speech, the awards, the farewell and the close. To be named.
Student MC 1

Class items

A Class 7 or 8 student with no role in the play. Introduces class items before and after the play.
Student MC 2

Alternates with MC 1

From a class that is free at the right moments. To be named.

Rules for every anchor: mic a fist below the chin; speak to the back row; never ad-lib a name (every name is on the card, with pronunciation); a link lasts no more than a minute; if the stage is late, say "The children are just getting ready, please bear with us," and wait.

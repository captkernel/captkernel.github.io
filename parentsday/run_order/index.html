Overview

Every child on stage, the little ones home first.

The whole school performs: seventeen class dances, the play The School of Time, in which Nursery to Class 6 each have a scene and Classes 7 and 8 play the teachers, and the school band. The order is built so that Nursery, LKG and UKG finish first, and so that every class has at least twenty minutes to change between its dance and its scene.

Juniors' day

11:00 → 1:44

Just under three hours from reporting to going home, with a long rest in the holding room during Scenes 3 to 9.
Holding rooms

Two adults each

Each junior holding room has its class teacher plus one helper. Children stay seated with their class until called.
Awards in costume

No change

The award ceremony follows the play directly, so winners come up in their play costume.
1:44 pm

Junior farewell: the handover

  1. 1:37: during the applause before the Chief Guest speaks, ushers quietly ask Nursery, LKG and UKG parents to move to the stage-side area.
  2. Junior class teachers bring their classes to the wings and then stand at the foot of the side steps.
  3. The teacher MC calls one class at a time: Nursery, then LKG, then UKG. The children cross the stage, wave to the hall, and go down the steps.
  4. The class teacher hands each child only to their own parent or the registered adult. One class on the steps at a time.
  5. A child not collected waits with the class teacher at the side of the stage, never in the aisle.
1:23 pm

Award ceremony

Basis

First Terminal 2026-27

Total marks from the First Terminal Examination (5 to 20 July). Top three in every class, Nursery to Class 8.
Ties

Shared ranks

Children with the same total share the rank, and each receives a trophy and a certificate. Expect 33 to about 40 awards.
Flow

~70 seconds a class

The MC names the class and its teacher, then third, second and first place. The Chief Guest hands trophies, the Principal hands certificates, then one group photo.

An award steward (one teacher) lines up each class's winners in the wings two classes ahead and carries the trophies in order. This keeps the ceremony to 14 minutes.

Anchors

MCs

Classes 7 and 8 are on stage for the whole play, so no senior can anchor both halves. A teacher leads all day, with two student co-anchors.

Lead MC · teacher

The whole day

Protocol, guest introductions, awards, Chief Guest, the junior farewell and the close. A confident English speaker who is not a class teacher. To be named.
Student MC 1

Class items

A Class 7 or 8 student with no role in the play. Introduces class items before and after the play.
Student MC 2

Junior and senior blocks

Suggested from Class 5, which is free except for Scene 4 and its dance at 2:15. To be named.

Rules for every anchor: mic a fist below the chin; speak to the back row; never ad-lib a name (every name is on the card, with pronunciation); if the stage is late, say "The children are just getting ready, please bear with us," and wait.

Overview

Every child on stage, the little ones home first.

The whole school performs: seventeen class dances, the play The School of Time (Nursery to Class 6 each have a scene, and Classes 7 and 8 play the teachers), and the school band. The day opens with a tribute to the school's founders. The order is built so that Nursery, LKG and UKG finish first, and every class has at least twenty minutes to change between its dance and its scene.

12:05 pm

Tribute to the founders

Kids Planet was founded in 2010 by Mrs Neeta Parmar, and built alongside her by Mr Ranjeet Parmar. The day begins with them.

  1. Before doors open: framed portraits of both founders stand on two easels at the side of the stage, garlanded, with a small table of flowers in front. They stay there all day; the children perform beside them.
  2. 12:05: the teacher MC introduces the tribute in two sentences: the founding, and that this year's welcome song is for them.
  3. As the choir begins, the Principal lays flowers before each portrait and stands beside them until the song ends.
  4. If the hall has a screen, the same two photographs appear side by side for the length of the song, with no caption.
  5. After the song, the MC thanks the choir and moves on. No speech, no minute's silence: it's a children's day, and the song is a welcome.

Awards in costume

No change

1:37 pm

Junior farewell: the handover

  1. While the last classes receive their awards, ushers quietly ask Nursery, LKG and UKG parents to move to the stage-side area.
  2. Junior class teachers bring their classes to the wings and then stand at the foot of the side steps.
  3. The teacher MC calls one class at a time: Nursery, then LKG, then UKG. The children cross the stage, wave to the hall, and go down the steps.
  4. The class teacher hands each child only to their own parent or the registered adult. One class on the steps at a time.
  5. A child not collected waits with the class teacher at the side of the stage, never in the aisle.
1:23 pm

Award ceremony

Basis

First Terminal 2026-27

Total marks from the First Terminal Examination (5 to 20 July). Top three in every class, Nursery to Class 8.
Ties

Shared ranks

Children with the same total share the rank, and each receives a trophy and a certificate. Expect 33 to about 40 awards.
Flow

~70 seconds a class

The MC names the class and its teacher, then third, second and first place. The Principal presents the trophy and certificate; the Vice Principal hands them across. One group photo.

An award steward (one teacher) lines up each class's winners in the wings two classes ahead and carries the trophies in order. This keeps the ceremony to 14 minutes.

Anchors

MCs

Classes 7 and 8 are on stage for the whole play, so no senior can anchor both halves. A teacher leads all day, with two student co-anchors.

Lead MC · teacher

The whole day

The welcome, the tribute, the awards, the junior farewell and the close. A confident English speaker who is not a class teacher. To be named.
Student MC 1

Class items

A Class 7 or 8 student with no role in the play. Introduces class items before and after the play.
Student MC 2

Junior and senior blocks

From a class that is free at the right moments. To be named.

Rules for every anchor: mic a fist below the chin; speak to the back row; never ad-lib a name (every name is on the card, with pronunciation); if the stage is late, say "The children are just getting ready, please bear with us," and wait.

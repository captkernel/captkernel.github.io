The house

Built to be read from the lane

Slate walls, limestone slabs and five gables. Inside, the shared rooms sit at the bottom and the top, with the bedrooms between them.

Ground floor · ±0.00 m

The Brick Room

Breakfast is cooked to order and served at one long table in the Brick Room. It is included in every rate.

The Brick Room is also reception, the place to leave a bag and where the kettle is always on. It keeps the only brick walls in the house.

Third floor · +9.90 m

The terrace

The third-floor terrace runs across the corner of the house under a white steel pergola. It has tables for breakfast on warm mornings and is open to every guest until 10 pm. Tea and coffee are served there on request.

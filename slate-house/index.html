The house

12 rooms, one long breakfast table

Slate House is a family-run bed and breakfast on a lane above the bazaar. The rooms are stacked the way the house is built: balcony rooms on the first two floors, family suites on the terrace floor and two lofts under the gables.

Everyone comes down to the same place in the morning. Breakfast is cooked to order in the Brick Room, the terrace is open to all, and nothing in the house is more than four flights from anything else.

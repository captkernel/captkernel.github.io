The two Lofts sit under the gables at the top of the house. The ceiling follows the roof, rising to just over three metres along the ridge and coming down to low storage along the eaves. The bed sits under the gable end, with a dormer window on each side of the room.

These are the rooms for people who like to be at the top of the stairs. There is no balcony, but the dormers look out over the roofs to the far ridge, and the terrace is one flight down.

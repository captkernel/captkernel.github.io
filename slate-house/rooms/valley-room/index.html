Valley Room

The house's standard room: a queen bed, a proper desk and a balcony wide enough for two chairs.

Four Valley Rooms run side by side across the first floor, one to each bay of the front. You come in past the bathroom and wardrobe, and the room opens up toward a glass door and the balcony beyond it.

The balcony sits behind a waist-high slate wall, so you can leave the door open and still feel tucked in. Morning sun reaches it by about eight. The first floor is the easiest climb in the house and the closest to breakfast.

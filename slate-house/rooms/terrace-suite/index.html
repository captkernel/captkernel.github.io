Terrace Family Suite

Two connected rooms on the terrace floor: a king bedroom, a twin room for children or friends, and a door straight onto the terrace.

Each Terrace Family Suite is two rooms joined by an inner door. The main bedroom has the king bed and a glass door onto the terrace. The second room has two single beds and a desk, and closes off for children who go to bed early.

The third floor is where the house changes from open bays to solid slate walls, so these are the quietest rooms on the front. The terrace outside is shared with other guests until 10 pm. After that it belongs to this floor.

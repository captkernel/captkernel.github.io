Balcony Suite

A larger room on the second floor with a king bed, a daybed that sleeps a third guest and a sitting corner by the glass.

The Balcony Suites sit directly above the Valley Rooms and share their four-bay layout, but each one is a third larger. The extra space goes to a sitting corner by the balcony door and a daybed along the wall, made up as a single bed when three of you are staying.

One floor higher makes a difference. From here the view clears the neighbouring roofs and you look straight down the valley. The bathroom has two basins and a larger shower.

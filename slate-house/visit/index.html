How to reach the house, when to come, and what to know before you book.

Directions

Getting here

12 Orchard Lane, Old Bazaar Road, Your Town 000000. Reception is staffed 7 am to 10 pm.

By road
Orchard Lane turns off Old Bazaar Road beside the post office. Follow it 200 m uphill and the house is on your left. The last stretch is narrow, so larger vehicles should stop at the bottom.
By bus
Overnight buses from the plains reach the town bus stand each morning. The house is 1.5 km from the stand, ten minutes by taxi.
By air
The nearest airport is 40 km away, about an hour and a quarter by road. We can book a taxi to meet you.
On foot
The bazaar is a ten-minute walk downhill. The walk back takes fifteen.

House rules

While you're here

  • Check-in is from 1 pm and check-out is by 11 am. Early arrival or a late start is free when the room is ready.
  • Every adult guest needs a government photo ID at check-in. Guests from abroad need a passport and visa.
  • The house is quiet from 10 pm to 7 am.
  • Smoking is allowed on balconies and the terrace, not inside.
  • Children of all ages are welcome. Under-threes stay free in a cot.
  • We're sorry, but we can't take pets.
  • Visitors who aren't staying are welcome in the Brick Room until 9 pm.
Booking

Booking and cancelling

To confirm
A deposit of 30% holds your room. The balance is paid at check-in.
Paying
UPI, bank transfer, cards and cash.
Cancelling
Free up to 7 days before arrival. Within 7 days the deposit is kept. Within 48 hours, or if you don't arrive, the first night is charged.
Peak dates
For peak-season stays, free cancellation ends 14 days before arrival.
Taxes
Rates include breakfast. GST is added at the current rate.
Questions

Things people ask

Is breakfast included?

Yes, for every guest, every morning. It's cooked to order between 7:30 and 10:30.

Do you serve other meals?

Dinner is a set menu served at 8 pm for guests who book it by noon. There is no lunch service, but the bazaar is ten minutes away.

Is there a lift?

Yes, from the ground floor to the third floor. The two Lofts are one more flight up by stairs.

Will the room be warm in winter?

Every room has a panel heater from October to March, wool quilts and hot water all day.

Can I arrive late at night?

Reception closes at 10 pm. Tell us your arrival time and someone will wait up.

Can you arrange taxis and day trips?

Yes. Ask at reception or mention it when you enquire, and we'll book a driver we know.

Can I book the whole house?

Yes. All twelve rooms sleep 32 guests. Write to us with your dates for a whole-house rate.

Is the Wi-Fi good enough to work on?

It handles video calls in the rooms and on the terrace. There is a desk in every room.

Still deciding?

Send your dates and we'll tell you what's free and which floor would suit you.
